## Problem

Sellers worked in Excel and uploaded products by hand. Mistakes were common.

## What I did

- Mapped a seller's full day from start to finish.
- Combined bulk upload and validation into a single screen.
- Built a small design system for the dashboard.

## Outcome

Adding products became twice as fast, with fewer errors.
