## Problem

Almost half of the people who started sign-up never finished. Nobody knew exactly why.

## What I did

- Talked to 12 users and watched where they got stuck.
- Merged document scanning into a single step.
- Tested the prototype twice and rewrote the copy in plain language.

## Outcome

Drop-off went down, and support requests were cut in half.
